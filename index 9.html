The story behind the service

The real reason I care about helping people show the world what they’re worth.

“My mind runs like a Formula One car. Sometimes like a Ferrari.”

I have dyslexia and ADHD. I process information at full speed, connect dots others don’t see, and jump between ideas faster than most people can keep up.

I'll be honest with you — my brain doesn't work like everyone else's.

For a long time, the world tried to tell me that was a problem. It wasn't. It was my superpower.

I was born and raised in Venezuela, where I trained as a paramedic — learning to stay calm in chaos, make fast decisions under pressure, and lead when everything around me was uncertain.

When my country changed and staying was no longer an option, I packed up everything, took my wife's hand, and moved to the UK. No safety net. No guarantee. Just resilience — and an unshakeable belief that I was built for more.

And then I rebuilt myself. Completely. From housekeeping to store operations. From data entry to running full purchase order cycles and my own e-commerce business across three platforms.

From zero to certifications in AI, Business Intelligence, Data, and Supply Chain — from Google, Anthropic, Cisco, and Coursera. All of it while working full-time. All of it self-driven.

My friends call me a genius. I just call it focus — Ferrari style.

Today I use everything I've learned — operations, AI, data, storytelling, and an obsession with innovation — to do one thing: help people like you show the world exactly what you're worth.

Because I know what it feels like to have everything inside you and not know how to get it out. I've been there. And I found the way. Now I'm here to help you find yours.
